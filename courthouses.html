Towson Courthouses

The seat of Baltimore County government · Circuit Court & District Court in Towson, MD 21204

Corner of Bosley & Chesapeake Avenues

Towson is the county seat of Baltimore County, Maryland — one of the state's oldest continuously used government centers. The county's courthouse complex is clustered just a few blocks east of 512 W Chesapeake Ave: the modern Baltimore County Courts Building (the Circuit Court) sits at the corner of Bosley and Chesapeake Avenues, faces the historic Baltimore County Courthouse (the "Old Courthouse") across a public plaza, and is joined by the Baltimore County Office Building and the county's District Court location on E. Chesapeake Ave. Reference: mdcourts.gov clerk directions.

The main Circuit Court for Baltimore County — general trial court handling major civil, criminal, family, and juvenile matters, plus land records, licensing, and the Register of Wills. Also called the "New Courthouse" to distinguish it from the historic 1855 building across the plaza. Handicapped access is via the ramp on the Pennsylvania Avenue side; metered parking is available on Pennsylvania and Baltimore Avenues, and multiple parking garages are nearby. Accessible by MTA routes 3, 8, 9, 11, and 55. Directions from I-695: take Exit 26 South (York Road), go 0.4 mi south and turn right onto Bosley Ave; the building is five traffic lights down, on the left.

Baltimore County's original 19th-century courthouse, an imposing Greek Revival building that still anchors the Towson government square. It houses county administrative offices today and hosts ceremonial functions. The building shares a plaza with the modern County Courts Building — a small piece of Towson history that's part of what makes the county-seat character of downtown Towson distinctive.

The Towson location of the District Court of Maryland — the state's limited-jurisdiction trial court for Baltimore County. Handles traffic citations, most misdemeanors, small-claims and landlord–tenant civil matters, and preliminary hearings. Sister Baltimore County District Court locations are in Catonsville and Essex. Note: state notices have flagged upcoming location changes for some Baltimore County District Court services — verify current location on the state courts website before visiting.

Home of the Baltimore County Executive, County Council chambers, and many of the county's operating departments (planning, permits, and licensing among them). Sits directly across Chesapeake Avenue from the courthouse block, forming the core of Towson's civic center — all within a comfortable walk of 512 W Chesapeake Ave.

Getting there from 512 W Chesapeake Ave

The property sits three-quarters of a mile west of the courthouse cluster on the same street — literally West Chesapeake Avenue. Head east on Chesapeake Ave and the county government center rises where Chesapeake meets Bosley (Circuit Court, County Office Building, Old Courthouse). Continue two blocks further east, cross York Road, and Chesapeake becomes East Chesapeake Avenue — the District Court is at 120 E. Chesapeake.

Approximate walking distances from 512 W Chesapeake Ave: County Office Building ~0.3 mi, Circuit Court & Old Courthouse ~0.4 mi, District Court ~0.7 mi.

By car from I-695 (Baltimore Beltway), the state's directions are: Exit 26 South onto York Road, travel 0.4 mi south, right onto Bosley Ave (the Towson Bypass), continue five traffic lights south to the County Courts Building on the left, at the corner of Bosley and Chesapeake Avenues.

By transit: MTA bus routes 3, 8, 9, 11, and 55 all serve downtown Towson and the courthouse block. MTA information: 410-539-5000.
