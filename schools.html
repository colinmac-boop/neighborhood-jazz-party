West Towson · Baltimore County Public Schools

Families at 512 W Chesapeake Ave fall in the Baltimore County Public Schools (BCPS) attendance zone for West Towson Elementary, Dumbarton Middle, and Towson High — three of Baltimore County's better-rated public schools. West Towson is also within easy driving distance of several of Maryland's top private schools, including Loyola Blakefield, Notre Dame Preparatory, Calvert Hall College, and the K–8 Immaculate Conception School. Frequently Asked Questions

What public school district is 512 W Chesapeake Ave in?
512 W Chesapeake Ave is served by Baltimore County Public Schools (BCPS): West Towson Elementary (K–5), Dumbarton Middle (6–8), and Towson High School (9–12).

What is the GreatSchools rating for West Towson Elementary?
West Towson Elementary is rated 9 out of 10 by GreatSchools and receives an A− from Niche, placing it among the top-rated public elementary schools in Baltimore County.

What are the closest private schools to 512 W Chesapeake Ave?
Immaculate Conception School (K–8, ~0.6 mi) is the closest, followed by Loyola Blakefield (6–12 boys, ~1.6 mi), Notre Dame Preparatory (6–12 girls, ~3.2 mi), and Calvert Hall College (9–12 boys, ~4.1 mi).

How much is private-school tuition in Towson?
Tuition ranges roughly from about $10,800/yr at Immaculate Conception School (Catholic K–8) to about $19,000–$22,500/yr at the major Catholic college-prep highs (Loyola Blakefield, Calvert Hall, Notre Dame Prep), and $32,000–$36,000/yr at top independent schools like Calvert School and Roland Park Country School.
