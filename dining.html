Towson Branch — Baltimore County Public Library (BCPL)

320 York Rd, Towson, MD 21204 · (410) 887-6166

Public library · Free · Distance: ~0.5 mi · BCPL system

The Towson Branch is a full-service Baltimore County Public Library location in downtown Towson, about half a mile from 512 W Chesapeake Ave. Amenities include free public Wi-Fi, meeting rooms and quiet study rooms, EV charging, passport services, fax and 3D printing, a Chinese-language collection, and a Spanish-language collection. It also hosts children's storytime, teen programs, author events, and community classes. The branch traces its origins to the Woman's Club of Towson in 1936 and moved into the current York Road building in 1974.
