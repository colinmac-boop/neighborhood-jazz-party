About the Neighborhood

512 W Chesapeake Ave is situated in West Towson, one of Baltimore County's most established residential neighborhoods. Located just minutes from downtown Towson, the Towson Town Center, and the Baltimore Beltway (I-695), the area combines suburban character with easy access to Baltimore City. The 21204 zip code includes parts of Towson, Ruxton, and Riderwood, and is known for tree-lined streets, historic homes, and strong public schools including West Towson Elementary, Dumbarton Middle School, and Towson High School.

The property at 512 West Chesapeake Avenue sits on a flat, fenced lot with off-street parking, within walking distance of local shops, restaurants, and Towson University. Baltimore County property records list this as APN 04-0916451090.
